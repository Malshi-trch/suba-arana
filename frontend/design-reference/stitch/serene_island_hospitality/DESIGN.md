## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the warmth, serenity, and timeless grace of traditional Sri Lankan hospitality. It caters to intentional travelers seeking immersive homestays, heritage villas, and eco-lodges tucked within central highlands, tea estates, and coastal sanctuaries.

The emotional signature balances peaceful retreat with cultural reverence. Modern, restrained layout principles intersect with artisanal warmth: spacious cream backdrops provide breathing room, while rich botanic greens, sacred golds, and subtle lotus motifs anchor the experience in place. The interface relies on warm minimalism enriched by editorial typography, delicate linear icons, high-craft cards, and soft ambient light, avoiding cluttered tourism tropes in favor of dignified calm.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the island’s natural and architectural heritage:

- **Primary (`#183B2B`)**: Deep Ceylon Forest Green, conveying quiet sanctuary, flora, and stability. Used for primary branding, structural headers, high-emphasis text, and grounding surfaces. A secondary forest tone (`#245447`) serves as hover states and secondary action surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#C69A3B` / `#D4AF37`)**: Temple Gold, referencing sacred architecture, brass oil lamps, and warm sunlight. Applied to verification badges, rating stars, featured highlights, and primary booking CTAs.
- **Tertiary (`#B85E3C`)**: Warm Terracotta, referencing clay tiles and pottery. Used sparingly for curated promotional flags, urgent alerts, and cultural callouts.
- **Highlight (`#287091`)**: Ocean Blue, evoking the Indian Ocean and water bodies. Applied to coastal tags, interactive badges, and amenity highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` & `#F7F4EE`)**: Warm soft cream surfaces replacing stark clinical whites, paired with `#1A201C` (Deep Charcoal) for effortless high-contrast typographic readability without harshness.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy bridges literary, historic grace with contemporary screen readability.

- **Headings & Display**: Set in `Playfair Display`. Its sculpted serifs and high contrast evoke classical Sri Lankan architectural elegance, high-end travel journalism, and warm hospitality. Large titles benefit from slightly tightened tracking.
- **Body & Functional UI**: Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Its open apertures and balanced proportions maintain supreme legibility across complex booking details, filters, and mobile cards.
- **Label Caps**: Used exclusively for metadata tags, status pills, and room categorization, always paired with generous tracking to retain clarity at compact sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 12-column grid anchors desktop views with generous outer margins (`3rem`) to create an airy, unhurried visual pace. Mobile experiences collapse into a 4-column system with tight `1.25rem` perimeter buffers to maximize imagery real estate.

Spacing adheres strictly to an 8px base rhythm. Section padding uses expansive vertical intervals (`3rem` to `5rem`), maintaining visual breathing room between curated stay collections and search components. Cards, list items, and internal search modules employ consistent internal offsets of `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px) for harmonious balance.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses soft tonal layering and warm ambient diffusion rather than harsh shadows:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base canvas sits on `#FAF7F2`. Elevated cards and search modules rest on pure `#FFFFFF` or elevated cream `#F7F4EE`.
- **Ambient Shadows**: Shadows are cast with a slight warm undertone: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(24, 59, 43, 0.06)` for static cards, elevating to `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(24, 59, 43, 0.12)` on hover.
- **Ghost Borders**: Interactive containers and inactive form fields feature delicate, translucent borders using `rgba(24, 59, 43, 0.08)` to frame items cleanly without visual weight.
- **Hero Overlays**: Image banners use subtle gradient scrims transitioning from transparent to `rgba(24, 59, 43, 0.65)` to preserve headline legibility over varied photography.

## Shapes

The design uses a refined rounded shape language (`roundedness: 2`). Standard controls, input containers, and small badges utilize `0.5rem` (8px) border radius. Larger structural units—such as property cards, destination tiles, and search bars—use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) to maintain a soft, friendly, and hospitable silhouette. Floating actions and filter pills transition to full capsules to provide tactile ergonomics on mobile touch surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary CTA**: Solid Temple Gold (`#C69A3B`) background with pure white text, transitioning to `#B3892F` on hover. Used for booking confirmations, primary searches, and reserve actions.
- **Secondary CTA**: Forest Green outline (`1.5px solid #183B2B`) with `#183B2B` typography, filling to solid green on hover.
- **Tertiary / Text Action**: Deep Charcoal text with subtle gold underline or directional arrow.

### Search Bar & Filters
- **Floating Global Search Bar**: Elevated white surface container with pill or soft rounded geometry. Divided into segmented interactive sections (Destination, Check-in, Check-out, Guests) partitioned by vertical ghost dividers (`1px solid rgba(24, 59, 43, 0.1)`).
- **Filter Chips**: Soft rounded capsules with `#FAF7F2` fill and subtle border. Active state shifts to Deep Forest Green fill with white text.

### Accommodation Cards
- **Structure**: Vertical or horizontal orientation with an image aspect ratio of 4:3 or 16:9. Images feature a subtle inset bookmark/favorite icon in the top-right corner.
- **Content Hierarchy**: 
  - Eyebrow location badge with gold map-pin icon.
  - Heading in `Playfair Display` with high-emphasis charcoal coloring.
  - Star rating with `#C69A3B` filled stars and review count in muted text.
  - Amenities presented as compact icon-label pairs (Free Wi-Fi, Pool, Mountain View).
  - Price anchor anchored to bottom right: bold numerical rate with `/ night` suffix, accompanied by a compact gold "Book Now" button.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields**: Soft warm white backgrounds with a crisp `1px` border in `#183B2B` (15% opacity). Focused state shifts border to Temple Gold with a soft 3px gold halo.
- **Selection Controls**: Custom circular radio buttons and rounded checkboxes in Forest Green fill when checked, featuring subtle checkmarks.

### Cultural Badges & Lotus Motifs
- **Heritage Badges**: Compact pills rendered in soft Terracotta (`#B85E3C` at 10% opacity) with solid terracotta text.
- **Brand Motifs**: Delicate, single-weight gold line-art lotus emblems deployed in headers, footers, and booking verification badges to evoke authenticity and peace.
